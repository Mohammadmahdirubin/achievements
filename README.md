## 🇬🇧 About

Mohammadmahdi Rubin is a Russian language educator, author, translator, researcher, and curriculum developer in Iran, with more than 25 years of experience in Russian language education and the development of educational materials.

His professional and academic work focuses on:

- Russian as a Foreign Language (RFL)
- Russian literature and culture
- Russian–Persian translation
- Translation studies
- Intercultural Communicative Competence (ICC)
- Curriculum development
- Academic and literary research

## 📖 Literary Translation | ترجمهٔ ادبی

### English

A major area of work is the translation of Russian literary and cultural texts into Persian, with particular attention to accuracy, literary style, terminology, names, and historical context.

Current and recent translation projects include works by Russian classical authors, including **S. T. Aksakov**.

### فارسی

یکی از حوزه‌های اصلی فعالیت محمدمهدی روبین، ترجمهٔ متون ادبی و فرهنگی روسی به فارسی است؛ با تأکید بر وفاداری به متن، حفظ سبک ادبی، دقت در اصطلاحات و نام‌های خاص و توجه به زمینهٔ تاریخی اثر.

از پروژه‌های ترجمهٔ جاری و اخیر می‌توان به ترجمهٔ آثار نویسندگان کلاسیک روس، از جمله **س. ت. آکساکوف**، اشاره کرد.

This repository provides a public academic and professional record of the achievements, publications, translations, research projects, conference papers, and professional activities of **Mohammadmahdi Rubin**.

It is intended as a continuously developing portfolio for researchers, educators, translators, publishers, and readers interested in Russian literature, Russian language education, and Russian–Persian cultural and literary exchange.
