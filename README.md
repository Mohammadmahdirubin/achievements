# Mohammadmahdi Rubin

Russian Language Educator · Researcher · Author · Translator · Curriculum Developer

## About

Mohammadmahdi Rubin is a Russian language educator, author, translator, researcher, and curriculum developer in Iran, with more than 25 years of experience in Russian language education and the development of educational materials.

His professional and academic work focuses on:

- Russian as a Foreign Language (RFL)
- Russian literature and culture
- Russian–Persian translation
- Translation studies
- Intercultural Communicative Competence (ICC)
- Curriculum development
- Academic and literary research

## Books & Educational Publications

Author and developer of more than 20 books and educational publications related to Russian language learning, Russian literature, Russian culture, and language education.

Selected works include:

- *Road to Russia 1–2*
- *Path to Success 1–2*
- *Russian Reading 1–4*
- *Encyclopedia of Russia*
- *Great Figures of Russian Literature*
- *Culture of Russia*
- *Cities of Russia*
- *Russian Orthodoxy*
- *Music of Russia*
- *Russian Handwriting*

## Literary Translation

A major area of work is the translation of Russian literary and cultural texts into Persian, with particular attention to accuracy, literary style, terminology, names, and historical context.

Current and recent translation projects include works by Russian authors such as **S. T. Aksakov** and other Russian classical writers.
